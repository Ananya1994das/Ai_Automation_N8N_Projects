# 📰 Daily AI News Automation Using n8n

## 📌 Project Overview

The Daily AI News Automation is an AI-powered workflow developed using n8n to fetch the top 5 AI-related news updates, generate concise summaries of each article, and deliver them directly to my email inbox every day at 9:00 AM.

Each news article is summarized into 5–6 informative sentences, designed to provide approximately a one-minute reading experience. This allows readers to understand important AI developments quickly without having to read multiple lengthy articles.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Automate daily AI news collection.
- Retrieve the top 5 AI-related news updates.
- Generate AI-powered summaries for each news article.
- Convert lengthy news articles into 5–6 sentence summaries.
- Provide an approximately one-minute reading experience per article.
- Deliver the curated news digest automatically through email at 9:00 AM.

## ⚙️ Workflow Process

1. **Schedule Trigger:** Initiates the workflow automatically every day at 9:00 AM.
2. **News Retrieval:** Fetches the latest AI-related news from the configured news source.
3. **Data Processing:** Processes the retrieved news and selects the top 5 updates.
4. **AI-Powered Summarization:** Uses an AI model to understand and summarize each news article into 5–6 meaningful sentences, creating an approximately one-minute read.
5. **Email Formatting:** Organizes the five news updates with their headlines, summaries, and relevant article links into a structured email.
6. **Email Delivery:** Automatically sends the final AI news digest to the configured email address.

## ✨ Key Features

- Fully automated daily execution.
- Retrieves the top 5 AI news updates.
- AI-powered article summarization.
- 5–6 sentence summaries for each article.
- Approximately one-minute reading experience per news update.
- Structured email containing headlines, summaries, and article links.
- Scheduled delivery every morning at 9:00 AM.
- Minimal manual intervention.

## 📊 Expected Output

An automatically generated daily email containing:

- **5 curated AI news articles**
- Individual headlines for each article.
- AI-generated summaries of 5–6 sentences.
- Approximately one-minute reading time per article.
- Relevant source links for further reading.
