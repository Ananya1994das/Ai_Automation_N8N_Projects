### 📰 Daily AI News Automation | n8n

An automated AI news digest built using n8n that fetches the top 5 AI-related news updates and delivers them directly to my email every day at 9:00 AM. This workflow eliminates manual news searching and keeps me updated on the latest developments in Artificial Intelligence through scheduled automation.
