# 📰 Daily AI News Automation Using n8n

## 📌 Project Overview

The Daily AI News Automation is an automated workflow developed using **n8n** to collect and deliver the top 5 AI-related news updates directly to my email inbox every day at 9:00 AM.

The primary objective is to automate daily AI news monitoring, reduce manual searching, and ensure consistent access to the latest developments in Artificial Intelligence.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Automate daily AI news collection.
- Identify and retrieve the top 5 AI news updates.
- Schedule automatic execution every day at 9:00 AM.
- Deliver a consolidated news digest directly to email.
- Reduce manual effort involved in tracking AI industry updates.

## ⚙️ Workflow Process

1. **Schedule Trigger:** Initiates the workflow automatically every day at 9:00 AM.
2. **News Retrieval:** Fetches the latest AI-related news from the configured news source.
3. **Data Processing:** Processes the retrieved news and selects the top 5 updates.
4. **Email Formatting:** Organizes the selected news into a readable email format.
5. **Email Delivery:** Automatically sends the final news digest to the configured email address.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **n8n:** Workflow automation and orchestration.
- **Schedule Trigger:** Daily workflow execution.
- **News Source / API:** AI news retrieval.
- **Data Processing:** Filtering and organizing news updates.
- **Email Integration:** Automated email delivery.

## ✨ Key Features

- Fully automated daily execution.
- Five AI news updates delivered per day.
- Scheduled delivery at 9:00 AM.
- Automated information retrieval and processing.
- Email-based news notifications.
- Minimal manual intervention.

## 📊 Expected Output

An email containing the top 5 AI news updates, delivered automatically to the configured inbox every morning at 9:00 AM.
